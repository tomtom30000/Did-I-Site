# Privacy Policy for Did I

Last updated: May 16, 2026

Did I is a private, local-first iPhone app.

The app does not require an account.
The app does not use ads.
The app does not use analytics.
The app does not track users.
The app does not send your checks, proof entries, notes, or photos to a server.

Your checks, proof entries, notes, reminders, and photos are stored locally on your device.

If you purchase Lifetime Unlock, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Did I does not receive your payment card details.

If you contact support by email, your email address and message will only be used to respond to your request.
